About

Hi, I'm Tracy.

I have always been interested in understanding what shapes us. How our experiences influence the way we see ourselves, how we connect with others, and the patterns we carry through life.

My work has given me the difficult privilege of walking alongside people during some of life's hardest moments. I have learned that people are complicated, life does not always make sense, and sometimes the things that helped us survive are the very things that keep us feeling stuck.

I believe there is something powerful about being truly understood. Not because someone has all the answers, but because you no longer have to walk through everything alone.

Warm, trauma-informed therapy for adults seeking steadiness, clarity, and compassionate support.

My Approach

Therapy that starts with connection and curiosity.

Connection comes first

Meaningful therapy starts with trust, being heard, and having room to be yourself without pressure to have the perfect words.

Understanding the why

We explore the patterns that once helped you get through life and how they may be shaping your experience now.

More than just talking

Depending on your needs, I draw from EMDR, trauma-informed care, cognitive approaches, and attachment-based perspectives.

Room for all of you

There is room here for the messy, painful, and complicated parts of life, and for humor, curiosity, and ordinary conversation too.

Getting Started

We will figure it out together, one step at a time.

You do not have to have everything figured out before starting therapy. We will talk about what is happening in your life, what you would like to change, and what feeling better might look like for you.

  1. 01
    Reach out. Share what is bringing you in and any questions you have.
  2. 02
    Initial consultation. We begin with a conversation to get to know each other and explore fit.
  3. 03
    First session. We look at your history, current challenges, and goals for treatment.
  4. 04
    Ongoing support. We move at a pace that feels right and adjust as life changes.
